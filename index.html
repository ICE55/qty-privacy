Privacy Policy

QTY Technology · Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QTY Technology ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when you contact us through WhatsApp, Telegram, our website chat or other messaging channels, and when our customer service system ("the Service") processes those conversations.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details provided by the messaging platform, such as your phone number or account ID, display name and profile language.
  • Message content you send to us, including text, images, files and other media, and the time they were sent.
  • Message status such as delivered and read receipts.
  • Business information you choose to share during the conversation, such as your company name, email, product requirements, quotations and orders.

2. How we use your information

  • To reply to your inquiries and provide customer service.
  • To prepare quotations, process orders and follow up on deliveries you request.
  • To keep a conversation history so that our staff can serve you consistently.
  • To generate reply suggestions with AI tools that assist our staff. These tools process message content only to provide the Service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for purposes unrelated to the Service.

3. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that are needed to run the Service, such as Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform), Telegram, cloud hosting and AI model providers, and only to the extent required. We may also disclose information when required by law.

4. Retention

We keep conversation records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal and accounting obligations. After that, they are deleted or anonymized.

5. Security

We use access control, encrypted connections and other reasonable measures to protect your information. Access is limited to authorized staff.

6. Your rights and data deletion

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information at any time. To request deletion, email coolxuhanbing@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include the phone number or account you used to contact us. We will complete the request within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also stop receiving messages from us by replying "STOP" or blocking our account.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page.
